HOUSING ACCELERATOR PROGRAM

Invest in housing projects. Earn a financial return.

The Housing Accelerator Program gives eligible investors the opportunity to lend capital to selected housing projects through a Development Partner Loan.

Each investment opportunity sits within the broader IGV housing platform, where project alignment, delivery, future demand and long-term financing are considered as part of one coordinated housing model.

Your capital helps move housing projects forward.

Your investment is structured to generate a financial return.

The IGV Difference

The project behind the loan matters.

Development lending carries risk. What differentiates the Housing Accelerator Program is the environment surrounding the project into which investor capital is being lent.

Every project sits inside the integrated IGV housing platform, where municipal alignment, construction delivery, buyer demand and long-term financing are addressed together before capital is ever deployed.

Project Alignment

Projects are selected and developed in response to identified housing opportunities, planning pathways and community objectives.

Speed & Cost

IGV Build Systems brings standardized designs, advanced manufacturing and defined construction methodologies to improve visibility around project cost, quality and delivery.

Structured Demand

IGVhope creates structured pathways to home ownership, helping establish qualified future demand for homes delivered through the platform.

Defined Exit

Projects are developed with a long-term financing pathway considered from the outset, creating greater visibility around how the development progresses beyond completion.

Why the Housing Accelerator Program Is Different

Construction finance is only as strong as the project around it

Traditional construction lending typically finances a project after many of its underlying risks have already been established.

IGV takes a different approach:

The Housing Accelerator Program sits within an integrated platform designed to address the fundamental drivers of project performance before and throughout construction.

  • Municipal alignment supports the development pathway.
  • IGV Build Systems creates greater speed and cost predictability.
  • IGVhope establishes structured future demand.
  • Long-term financing creates a defined pathway beyond construction.

Capital is therefore deployed into a coordinated housing system rather than an isolated construction project.

Built Around Four Sources of Certainty

Greater certainty across the housing lifecycle

Traditional construction finance is exposed to uncertainty at multiple stages; approvals, construction cost, delivery timelines, occupancy and exit.

The IGV platform brings together four sources of certainty to reduce these variables before and during the deployment of capital.

Municipal Alignment

The Result
Four interconnected systems. One coordinated investment model.

Together, these elements create a housing platform designed to provide greater visibility across the factors that most directly influence residential development performance: approval, cost, delivery, demand and exit.

Capital With Purpose

The objective of the Housing Accelerator Program is not simply to finance construction.

It is to help create a repeatable housing delivery platform capable of bringing together land, approvals, construction, homeowners and capital in a more coordinated way.

How Projects Are Selected

Not every project is offered. Only those that meet our criteria.

Projects are assessed through a rigorous process before a Development Partner Loan opportunity is made available to investors.

  • Alignment with IGV platform and investment mandate
  • Municipal partnership and planning status
  • Construction methodology and cost certainty
  • Structured demand through IGVhope
  • Defined long-term financing pathway
  • Experienced Development Partner
  • Environmental, social and governance considerations

Secure. Structured. Transparent.

Secure

Development Partner Loans are secured by real estate and structured to protect investor capital.

Structured

Each opportunity is clearly documented with defined terms, use of funds, and conditions.

Transparent
